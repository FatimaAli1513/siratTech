## Privacy & Data Safety (Play Store Answers)

### Does the app collect any user data?
**No.** The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or usage data.

### Data types collected / shared
**None.** No name, email, location, device ID, or analytics.

### Why no data?
The app only shows static company information and service descriptions. The only actions are opening the device’s email app or browser when the user taps the email or website link. No data is sent to the developer from the app.

### Data safety form (Google Play Console)
- **Data collection:** No, the app does not collect any user data.  
- **Data sharing:** No.  
- **Data encryption:** N/A (no data collected).  
- **Users can request data deletion:** N/A (no data collected).  
- **Committed to Google Play Families Policy:** If targeting children – not applicable; app is for general/business audience.
